Anutam Srinivasan

Hi I'm Anutam Srinivasan, a Robotics Ph.D. student in the Trustworthy Robotics Lab at Georgia Tech advised by Prof. Glen Chou.

Research Statement: To ensure trustworthy autonomy in an uncertainty-fraught world, it is essential to mitigate uncertainty propagation in the machine learning-driven autonomy loop. To this end, I am developing theoretically grounded frameworks to actively reduce uncertainty across the robotics stack, encompassing perception, controls, and reasoning.

Undergraduate (The Ohio State University)

During my undergraduate studies, I double majored in Electrical & Computer Engineering and Mathematics. I was also fortunate to work with several encouraging mentors and advisors on projects that have shaped my current research outlook:

  • Under the advisement of Prof. Aaron Nielsen (Air Force Institute of Technology) I developed physics-informed machine-learning approaches to remove magnetic noise for magnetic anomaly navigation in GPS-contested environments.
  • I co-developed a framework for algorithmically Fair Conformal Prediction, a UQ technique, under the guidance of Dr. Pranav Maneriker (Dolby Labs), and Prof. Srini Parthasarathy (Ohio State).
  • I also worked with Prof. Kannan Athreya (Ohio State) to develop methods to identify and mitigate signal leakage on wireless communications from phones connected to the powerline.
